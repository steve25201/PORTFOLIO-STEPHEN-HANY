about

how I work

I start from the idea, build it into a full visual system, and follow it through to the applied identity. Brands should feel human, not generic.

I care about strong concepts, storytelling and clean execution. I like work that feels fun and approachable, and I steer away from anything corporate, sterile or generic.

I’m growing from executing designs toward shaping brand strategy, so each project starts with what the brand stands for before it gets a logo.

brief

PLATO is a B2B SaaS brand designed for restaurant owners who need smarter tools to run their business. Built around AI-powered features including Instant Reports, Split Pay, and QR Ordering, the brand combines technical precision with a human, approachable feel.

The identity reflects trust, simplicity, and connection, creating a visual language that feels both confident and easy to work with.

sedge

brief

Sedge is a full brand identity designed for a medical marketing agency. Built to communicate clarity, professionalism, and trust, the identity balances a clean, clinical precision with a warmer, more human visual language, avoiding the cold, sterile look many healthcare-adjacent brands default to.

The result is a system that feels credible to medical clients while staying approachable and modern.
